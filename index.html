Saturday hike

Mount Wismer Loop

We leave at 9:00 AM and we’re back for lunch. The trail is slightly challenging and it will be cold.

  • Sneakers or hiking boots with grip
  • Warm layers: base layer, fleece or sweatshirt, jacket
  • Hat and gloves
  • Water bottle

Our first hike with a group this big! We’ll go at a pace that works for everyone. Stay with the group and look out for each other.

Packing list

What to bring

Linens are provided, so no bedding needed. Bring a sleeping bag if you’d be more comfortable.

  • Towel
  • Toiletries
  • Warm layers and comfy clothes
  • Shoes for hiking
  • Water bottle
  • Bible, journal, and pen
  • Any medications you need
  • Phone charger
Meals & snacks

All meals are covered, from Friday dinner through Sunday breakfast, plus snacks during free time.

Allergy or dietary need we don’t know about? Let us know as soon as possible.

Bring your own snacks and board games! Some of our best moments happen during free time at night.
